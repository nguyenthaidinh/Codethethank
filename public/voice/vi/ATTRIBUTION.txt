THE UNWRITTEN LESSON — Vietnamese synthetic narration

Voice: vi_VN-vais1000-medium, distributed by Michael Hansen / rhasspy.
Model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/vi/vi_VN/vais1000/medium/MODEL_CARD
Model repository metadata: MIT. See upstream model card for dataset terms.
Dataset: VAIS-1000 Vietnamese Speech Synthesis Corpus.

Dataset license stated in model card: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Adaptation: project-authored game dialogue was synthesized offline with
piper-tts 1.8.0, normalized to -18 LUFS, then encoded as mono 22.05 kHz MP3.
These seven clips are synthetic opening narration, not recordings of a cast
or an imitation of any named person. Character conversations are subtitles only.
No player letter or recipient name is processed by a speech service.

The model weights and TTS engine are build tools and are not shipped here.
Unmodified upstream MODEL_CARD is included beside this attribution.
